# 3. CAMPAIGNS — FROM OFFER TO PUBLISHED CAMPAIGN

**[SCREEN: Campaigns → New Campaign]**

**HOST:**

Campaigns are the foundation of AutoAffiliate360.

Instead of starting with a blank page, you start with **one decision**: what are you going to promote?

**[SCREEN: New Campaign → Step 1 — Import Offer]**

### Step 1 — Import Offer (two ways in)

**Path A — You already have an offer**

**[SCREEN: “Paste links” — JV / sales page + affiliate hop link]**

Paste your **sales page URL** and your **affiliate hop link**. Click to extract — the system pulls the product details and kicks off the campaign. No copying long sales letters by hand.

**Path B — You don’t have an offer yet**

**[SCREEN: “Find by keyword” — search field → results list]**

Switch to **Find by keyword**. Type a niche or topic — like *AI automation*, *keto*, *email marketing* — and search.

AutoAffiliate360 searches affiliate marketplaces (such as **JVZoo**, **WarriorPlus**, and **ClickBank**), surfaces real products you can promote, and helps you **pick one** that fits.

**[SCREEN: Select a result → add hop link → Extract & continue]**

Choose the offer you like, add your **hop link** when you have it, then **Extract & continue**.

From there, it’s the **same streamlined wizard** for everyone:

**Offer → Knowledge Base → pages, emails, bonuses, traffic → Publish.**

You’re not stuck at “I don’t know what to sell.” You can go from **keyword to live campaign** in one flow — plug in what you have, or let the system **find the offer for you**, then AI builds everything around it.
